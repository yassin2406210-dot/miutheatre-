Our Mission

More Than a Club — A Community

The MIU Theatre Club exists to nurture artistic talent, build confidence, and create a space where students can express themselves freely through the performing arts.

We believe theatre is not just entertainment — it's about empathy, communication, and human connection. Every member becomes part of a family.

From beginner workshops to full-scale productions, we give every student the opportunity to discover their potential.

"All the world's a stage, and all the men and women merely players."
— William Shakespeare

Our Journey

A History of Performance

From a small idea in the OOA room to a fully established university club.

2016

The Beginning

A small group gathered in the OOA room with one dream — to create a theatre community at MIU.

2017

First Official Workshop

We hosted our first public acting workshop, drawing over 30 students.

2018

First Major Production

Our debut full-scale production performed to a sold-out university audience.

2020

Going Digital

We launched online rehearsals and virtual workshops to keep the community alive.

2022

Back on Stage

A powerful comeback production with our largest cast yet.

2025

120+ Members & Growing

Today MIU Theatre Club is one of the most active student organizations at the university.

Acting Interview

Prove Your Craft

Apply for a formal acting interview. For serious performers committed to Arabic theatre.

⚠️ Requirements Strong Arabic reading skills and full commitment to the club's schedule are required.

Acting Interview Application

Apply for a formal acting interview with our directors.

Please confirm all commitments:

I have strong Arabic reading skills and am prepared to read scripts in Arabic during the interview.
I commit to attending all rehearsals, workshops, and productions as required.
I understand this is a serious commitment and will notify the club of any conflicts in advance.
🎬

Application Submitted!

Our directors will review your application and schedule your interview. Check your MIU email for updates.
